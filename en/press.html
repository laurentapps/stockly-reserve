Home stock, groceries and the return from shopping in one app.

Stockly Reserve helps families know what they already have, what they need to buy, what they picked up in store, and what goes back into the right storage place.

Why Stockly Reserve stands out

A complete flow

The app connects food inventory, grocery lists, in-store shopping and putting items away, instead of treating each moment separately.

Practical waste reduction

Expiry dates, quantities, opened products and alerts help families use what is already at home before buying again.

Guided experience

Built-in information pages and contextual help explain key screens: list, shopping, inventory and settings.

Key facts

PlatformiPhone, iOS
Main useHome stock, expiry dates, grocery lists and shopping
Inventory modeCompares the real content of a storage place with the saved app stock
Pro modeInventory, prices, statistics, export, custom storage places and removed limits
PrivacyNo required Stockly account, local data and optional iCloud sharing
AudienceFamilies and households that want a clearer everyday food reserve

Stockly Reserve was designed for a very concrete moment: opening the fridge, planning groceries, shopping, then putting everything away without losing track.
